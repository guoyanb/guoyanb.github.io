Yanbu Guo received the Ph.D. degree from Yunnan University, Kunming, China, and received a B.S. degree from Zhengzhou University of Light Industry, Zhengzhou, China. He is currently an Associate Professor with College of Software Engineering, Zhengzhou University of Light Industry, Zhengzhou, China, and also a visiting scholar with Southeast University, Nanjing, China. The interests mainly focus on the research topics:

  • Complex Biological Networks and Systems: Theory and Application of Artificial Intelligence
  • Neural Networks: Graph/Bayesian/Quantum Neural Networks
  • Biomedical Informatics: Biomolecule–Disease Associations/Drug Repositioning
  • Image Processing: Image Denoising/Enhancement/Fusion

Email: guoyanbu#zzuli.edu.cn (guoyanbu#gmail.com)
