Dr. Yanbu Guo received the Ph.D. degree from Yunnan University, Kunming, China, and received a B.S. degree from Zhengzhou University of Light Industry, Zhengzhou, China. He is currently an Associate Professor with College of Software Engineering, Zhengzhou University of Light Industry, Zhengzhou, China, and also a visiting scholar with Southeast University, Nanjing, China. His research interests include Complex Biological Networks and Systems, Neural Networks (Graph / Bayesian / Quantum Neural Networks), Biomedical Informatics (Biomolecule–Disease Associations / Drug Repositioning), and Image Processing (Image Denoising / Enhancement / Fusion).

Dr. Guo has published multiple papers in prestigious journals and conferences. His research has been supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Henan Provincial Key R&D and Promotion Special Project.

His research group is now recruiting self-motivated Undergraduate / Master / PhD students in Artificial Intelligence, Bioinformatics, and Medical Image Processing. He is also open to various collaborations. If you are interested in, please get in touch with him.
